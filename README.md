# FreeRails

[![License: GPL-3.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-GPL--3.0-blue.svg)](LICENSE.txt)

FreeRails is an open-source railway strategy and management game inspired by the famous Sid Meier's strategy games
*Railroad Tycoon* and *Railroad Tycoon II* from the 1990s. Turn a growing rail network into a business: choose
where to build, which services to run, and how to invest as the world and the economy change. FreeRails takes that strategic
foundation as inspiration for an independent open-source railway management game in the spirit of its predecessors.

Building and operating a railroad company is at the heart of play, with connected choices around terrain, track and stations
placement, cargo, train services, company finances, and competition. FreeRails aims for engaging strategic decisions rather
than realistic simulation, and for rules and presentation that make the complexity of a railroad easy to understand.

The project is written in Java and includes a desktop client and game server; development is ongoing.

The project continues a long-running series of FreeRails and Railz projects. Read the [project history](docs/user/history.md)
for background.
